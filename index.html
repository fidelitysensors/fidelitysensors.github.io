Services

Where Expertise Meets Engineering

Architecture-to-Target Matching

The right sensing architecture depends on the target analyte, the sample matrix, and the performance requirements — and the wrong choice compounds every downstream problem. I help teams evaluate and select the architecture most likely to succeed before significant resources are committed.

Design for Manufacturability

A biosensor that works in the lab but can't be made consistently at scale isn't a product. I work with development teams to identify manufacturability constraints early, align design choices with process capabilities, and reduce the gap between prototype performance and production yield.

Limit-of-Detection Optimization

Reaching the required LOD is often the central challenge in biosensor development. I bring a systematic approach to signal amplification, background suppression, and assay optimization — grounded in the physics of the measurement, not just empirical iteration.

Noise & Interference Mitigation

Real-world samples are complex. Non-specific binding, matrix effects, electrochemical interferences, and environmental variables can all degrade performance in ways that aren't visible in clean buffer. I help teams anticipate, characterize, and engineer around these challenges.

Approach

I work with teams at all stages: from early feasibility and architecture selection through to troubleshooting performance gaps in late-stage development. The goal is always the same — a sensor that works reliably, can be made consistently, and meets the performance requirements that actually matter.

Engagements are structured around your specific challenge, whether that's a focused technical review, ongoing advisory support, fractional technical leadership, or hands-on collaboration with your development team.
